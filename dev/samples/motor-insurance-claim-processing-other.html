Motor Insurance Claim Processing

End-to-end motor claim journey spanning 7 swimlanes: intake validation, automated triage, straight-through processing, fraud evaluation, repair coordination, and finance settlement.

FAST-TRACK PATH

Straight-Through Processing

  • Qualification: Estimated value below £5,000 and low fraud-risk assessment.
  • Zero Touch: Automated dispatch directly from Claims System to Repair Partner without handler intervention.
  • Rejection Fail-Safe: Partner rejection automatically reroutes case back to Claims Handler for partner reassignment.

GOVERNANCE & EXCEPTIONS

Manual Review & Fraud Triage

  • Evidence SLA: Claims Handler can request documentation from Customer; 14-day SLA timeout closes incomplete drafts.
  • Fraud Referral: High-risk flags mandate referral to Fraud Team (Cleared, Further Investigation, or Confirmed Fraud).
  • Invoice Variance: Overruns <=10% require handler review; overruns >10% trigger mandatory invoice revision.

SETTLEMENT & SLA

Finance & Closure

  • Automated Payment: Disbursed upon invoice approval with automated retry up to 2x on failure.
  • Fallback: Persistent payment failures generate manual Finance remediation tasks.
  • Closure: Payment confirmation triggers automated claim settlement and customer notification.
