Shortcuts & Interaction
  • Drag a Shape : Hover Shapes in the bottom bar, then click a shape or drag it onto the canvas (or onto an arrow). Flow holds Start, End, Step and Decision; Classic the flowchart symbols: Subprocess (a process documented elsewhere), Data (input/output), Document, Database (stored data) and Delay (a wait)
  • Drop onto an Arrow : Inserts the step between the two steps it joins and tidies the layout (also works for a step with no arrows yet)
  • Click or Drag Port : Hover a step to show its four anchor points (on a touch screen, select the step), then click or drag one to connect two steps with an arrow
  • Double-Click Arrow : Add or edit the arrow label (an arrow with no label draws nothing at its midpoint)
  • Click Arrow : Select it: a small bar at its midpoint (beside the label) offers Connector, Label and Delete; one Ctrl+Z brings a deleted arrow back
  • Connector (under Shapes, Flow) : Drag it onto a long arrow to break that arrow into a lettered pair, A beside its source and A beside its target; nothing else moves. Or click it to split the selected arrow (with none selected, click the arrow next; Esc cancels). The Connector button on a selected arrow does the same, and offers to join an existing pair that continues at the same step
  • Re-point a Pair : Drag a circle onto a step to move that end there. Or select the short arrow at a circle: its handle at the circle end moves the circle the same way, and any arrow's arrowhead dropped on a circle sends that arrow into the pair
  • Connector : Hover a lettered circle (or a step wired to one) to light its partner; click it to jump there (also when presenting). Drag a circle onto a step to move that end of the pair: the flow then continues at that step (or comes from it). Double-click to change the letter of the whole pair; Delete joins the arrow back together
  • Select Several Steps : Ctrl/Cmd+click adds a step to the selection or takes it out; Shift+click selects the steps along the arrows from the step clicked last; Shift+drag on blank canvas draws a box (with Ctrl/Cmd too, each step it touches is added or taken out); Ctrl/Cmd+A selects every step; Esc or a click on blank canvas clears it
  • Delete : Delete the selected step, steps, arrow or lane (or the bar's Delete button, shown while something is selected, with the count when several steps are). One Ctrl+Z brings them all back
  • Click Lane Name : Select the lane: a bar appears over its name with the lane's colour (the dots; the first, empty one gives it back the colour for its position), Rename, move earlier or later (up and down, left and right in a top-to-bottom diagram) and delete. The lane's icon comes from the AI or is guessed from its name. Delete asks to confirm and removes it with its steps and their arrows (undo brings them back; the last lane stays)
  • Drag Node : Drop a step into another lane (or onto an arrow). The layout tidies itself after every step you add, move or delete (Ctrl+Z undoes both at once), except a shape dragged onto blank canvas, which stays where you drop it until you connect it; with it off, steps stay where you drop them and snap to other steps' and lane centers. With several steps selected, drag any of them to move them all (each keeps its lane offset); Alt+↑/↓ moves the selection one lane up or down (←/→ in a top-to-bottom diagram)
  • Move Steps in the Flow : Select a step, or a run of steps with one way in and one way out (Shift+click its first and last step), and drag it onto another arrow while holding Alt: the run moves there and the gap it leaves closes up. Alt+←/→ (↑/↓ top to bottom) moves it one step earlier or later along a straight part of the flow. Without Alt, dragging a connected step never rewires it
  • Reorder Steps : Rewire the arrows (drag an arrow's end to another step, or delete and redraw it). Arrow edits never re-arrange the diagram, so it holds still while you rewire; press Tidy (or edit a step) to lay out the new order
  • Tidy : Lays the diagram out once, now
  • Note (under Shapes, Annotate) : Drag it onto a step to give that step a note, or click it to add one to the selected step. Dropped anywhere else, or clicked with nothing selected, it makes a free note (see below)
  • N : Add a note to the selected step, or a free note at the centre of the view when nothing is selected (or click the + chip on the corner of a hovered or selected step); Enter starts a new line, Ctrl+Enter, Esc or a click elsewhere finishes, an empty note is removed
  • Link a Note : One note can explain several steps. Select the note, then drag its link handle (bottom-right corner) onto a step, or Shift+click the step; do the same on a linked step to unlink it
  • Free Note : A note linked to no step stays where you put it, always on show, with no leader or badge; Auto-Tidy never moves it. Drag its link handle onto a step to link it (it then follows that step); an anchored note cannot be made free by unlinking
  • Note Badge : A step with a note shows a small badge: hover the badge (or the step) to peek at the note, click it to edit
  • Notes : Show or hide every note at once; they are fitted into the gaps between steps (saved with the file). Drag a note to place it yourself: it then follows its step. Double-click to edit, Delete removes the selected note
  • Subprocess Tabs : A large flow can be split into subprocess flows, one tab each (the folder tabs under the header: one box per level on the way down, the current flow's own subprocesses in the innermost; click a tab in an outer box, or press Alt+Up, to go back up). A Subprocess added from Shapes gets its own tab (start, one step, end) to fill in, down to four levels (the Subprocess shape is greyed out on level 4). A subprocess step is a step with a + in its top-left corner; when it has a flow, the + turns blue: click it, or the tab, to open it; a tab further out goes back to the step that led down. Deleting a subprocess that has content asks: Merge into parent (the steps take its place), Delete everything, or Cancel; right-click a subprocess tab for the same Delete. A lane is one actor in every tab: rename or recolour it anywhere. Each tab keeps its own zoom and Tidy; Export takes the tab on screen, or every tab as its own SVG
  • Collapse / Expand : Select two or more steps in one run (one way in, one way out, no loop-back across the edge) and click Collapse to move them into a new subprocess flow, named by typing; select a subprocess step and click Expand to put its flow back in place. Both are one undo step
  • Vertical / Horizontal : Switch between a left-to-right and a top-to-bottom layout (lanes become columns); saved with the file
  • Show lanes : In a diagram drawn without swimlanes, the Lane button reads Show lanes and turns them back on (name the actor, then add more lanes)
  • Drag Lane Name : Drag a lane's name pill to reorder swimlanes (a blue line shows where it will land), or use the move buttons in its bar; double-click the name to rename
  • Double Click : Edit step text, lane names, or edge conditions
  • Ctrl + S : Save changes directly into HTML
  • Ctrl + F or / : Search the diagram's text. Enter / Shift + Enter (or the arrow buttons) go to the next / previous match, Esc closes the search
  • Export (top right): download the whole diagram as an SVG or a 1.5x PNG, or copy it to the clipboard as a 1.5x PNG
  • Ctrl + Z / Ctrl + Y : Undo / redo the last edit (also Ctrl + Shift + Z), or the buttons at the bottom left
  • More (⋯, top right) : Paste Mermaid (replace the diagram from Mermaid text; Ctrl + V on the canvas does the same), Dark mode, and this list of shortcuts (?)
  • Present (top right) or ?present in the URL : a read-only view with only the zoom controls. Pan, zoom, hover and click still work; Esc or the Present button exits
  • Drag Canvas : Pan viewport
  • Scroll Wheel : Scroll the canvas up / down (Shift + wheel: left / right)
  • Ctrl + Scroll Wheel : Zoom in / out at the cursor
  • Touch : drag with one finger to pan, pinch with two to zoom. A drag that starts on an item pans unless the item is already selected; tap an item to select it, then drag it
