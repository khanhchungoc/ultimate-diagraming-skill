# Brief: Employee onboarding, end to end (swimlane process flowchart)

Actors: Hiring Manager, Recruiter, Candidate, HR Operations, Background Check Vendor, IT Service Desk, Facilities, Payroll, Security Office, Training Team.

1. The Hiring Manager raises a hiring request with role, grade and budget code. HR Operations checks the request against approved headcount; if no headcount exists, the request goes to Finance approval inside HR Operations (budget holder sign-off required above grade 7); if Finance rejects, the request is closed as "Hiring Request Rejected".
2. The Recruiter drafts the job ad, the Hiring Manager approves the ad (if not approved, the Recruiter revises it and resubmits), and the Recruiter publishes it on the careers site and two job boards.
3. The Recruiter screens applications within 5 working days, shortlists candidates and schedules interviews. The Hiring Manager runs two interview rounds; after each round the Hiring Manager decides whether the candidate advances. A rejected candidate receives a rejection email from the Recruiter and the process ends as "Candidate Rejected".
4. For the selected candidate, the Recruiter checks salary against the band. Offers above the band need HR Director approval (an HR Operations step); if declined, the Recruiter renegotiates with the Candidate and re-checks the band.
5. The Recruiter issues the offer letter. The Candidate accepts, declines or counters. A decline ends as "Offer Declined"; a counter-offer loops back to the band check. On acceptance, the Candidate signs the contract electronically.
6. HR Operations orders a background check from the Background Check Vendor. The vendor verifies identity, right to work, employment history (last 5 years) and criminal record, and returns a report within 10 working days. If the report shows an adverse finding, HR Operations reviews it with Legal guidance; if the finding is disqualifying, the offer is withdrawn ("Offer Withdrawn"), otherwise HR Operations records an exception.
7. Once cleared, these run in parallel: IT Service Desk creates the user account, assigns a laptop from stock (orders one if out of stock, 3-day SLA) and enrols the device in MDM; Facilities assigns a desk and orders a building access card; Payroll sets up the employee record, bank details and tax code; Security Office issues the security briefing pack. All four must complete before Day 1.
8. HR Operations sends the welcome email with the Day 1 agenda. On Day 1 the Candidate (now employee) collects the access card from Facilities, receives the laptop from IT, and signs the IT acceptable-use policy.
9. The Training Team enrols the employee in mandatory training (code of conduct, data protection, security awareness) due within 30 days. If training is overdue at day 30, the Training Team sends a reminder; if still overdue at day 45, the Hiring Manager is notified and access is suspended until completion.
10. At day 90 the Hiring Manager runs the probation review. Pass ends as "Probation Passed"; an extension adds 30 days and repeats the review once; a fail goes to HR Operations for termination processing, ending as "Employment Terminated".
